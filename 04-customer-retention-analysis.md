# Customer Retention & Revenue Analysis

## What it is
An analysis of public or synthetic transaction data that explains repeat purchasing, customer cohorts, time-to-second-purchase, revenue concentration, and changes in customer activity. Turn the findings into a short, decision-oriented report or interactive view.

## Why build it
This is a familiar business problem with a clear path from analysis to action. It demonstrates that you can translate data into questions a sales or finance team can use, rather than stopping at charts or model scores.

## Skills it highlights
- SQL, data cleaning, and analytical data modeling
- Cohort analysis, retention metrics, and segmentation
- Visualization and concise business storytelling
- Metric definitions and careful interpretation

## Suggested deliverable
Use a documented public dataset or clearly marked synthetic data. Include reusable SQL, a concise dashboard or report, definitions for each metric, and a prioritized set of findings or follow-up questions.

## Evidence of quality
Show how you handle one-time customers, incomplete transaction histories, refunds, and cohort maturity. Separate observed patterns from causal claims; recommend experiments where the data cannot establish causation.
