How to interpret this

Patterns, not explanations.

These observations are descriptive and do not establish causes of churn. This non-contractual dataset does not tell us why a customer stopped buying. Histories begin and end within the source window, so both earlier and later purchases may be missing. Anonymous transactions are excluded from customer measures; cancellation lines are treated as refunds in the full report, not as new purchases. Many customers are wholesalers.
